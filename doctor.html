Meet Dr. Renuka Patil

Skin and hair care with a personal touch.

Dr. Renuka Patil

Dr. Renuka Patil is a registered homoeopathic practitioner with the Maharashtra Council of Homoeopathy (Registration No. 65127), registered in 2017. She completed a certificate programme in Skin & Venereal Diseases at Unique Medical Foundation, Pune, in 2015. She provides consultation, treatment guidance, and personalized advice for various skin and hair problems, with individual attention to every patient’s concerns and needs. The clinic is open six days a week and remains closed on Saturdays.

  • Treatment and advice for skin and hair problems
  • Personal consultation for every patient
  • Open six days a week, closed on Saturday
